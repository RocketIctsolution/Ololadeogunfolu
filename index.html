About Ololade

Ololade Mary Ogunfolu is an accomplished Interior Designer, Builder, Business Strategist, Real-Life Coach, Mentor, and Entrepreneur with over 12 years of experience transforming spaces, building businesses, and empowering people to discover and live out their purpose.

She is the Owner of Lomark Furniture Center, a brand focused on providing timeless furniture and premium building materials sourced from Turkey and China, serving homeowners, interior designers, and construction professionals.

As an experienced Interior Designer and Builder, Ololade specialises in transforming residential and commercial spaces through thoughtful interior design, renovations, and construction. Her approach combines creativity, functionality, timeless aesthetics, and practical solutions to bring exceptional spaces to life.

Beyond design and construction, Ololade is passionate about business, personal development, identity, and purpose. Through her work as a business strategist, coach, and mentor, she helps entrepreneurs and professionals build purpose-driven businesses and lives, align their careers with their God-given purpose, and overcome confusion and distractions through practical strategies, mentorship, coaching, and prayer.

She is also the Founder of Learn With Lomark, an initiative dedicated to equipping aspiring interior designers with practical skills, industry knowledge, and business principles through hands-on training and mentorship.

Through Lomark Podcast and her digital content, Ololade shares practical insights on interior design, construction, entrepreneurship, business, faith, personal growth, and her real-life experiences — using her journey, lessons, victories, and challenges to inspire and equip others.

At the heart of everything she does is a desire to build spaces, businesses, and people — while helping others discover their identity, pursue excellence, and fulfil God's purpose for their lives. Ololade Mary Ogunfolu is more than an interior designer or business owner; she is a builder of spaces, businesses, and people.

Featured Book

Beyond The Pain
The Story of Ololade Ogunfolu

Beyond The Pain is Ololade Mary Ogunfolu's own story — an honest account of the seasons that tested her, and the faith, purpose and resolve that carried her through them.

Written from lived experience, it is an invitation to every reader walking through their own difficult season: pain is not the end of the story — it can become the very ground your purpose is built on.
